⚠️ 1. Problem Statement

Agricultural marketing in India is characterized by geographical fragmentation across thousands of APMC (Agricultural Produce Market Committee) mandis. Smallholder farmers and agricultural traders face significant challenges due to:

  • Information Asymmetry: Farmers lack visibility into price patterns in neighboring or inter-state mandis, resulting in distress selling at below-market rates.
  • Intra-Market Volatility: Daily fluctuations between minimum and maximum auction prices (Price Spread) are volatile and poorly documented.
  • Absence of Pattern Intelligence: Traditional portals display isolated tabular numbers but do not group mandis by cohesive economic behavior, leaving stakeholders without macro-level market segmentation.

🎯 2. Project Objectives

Objective A: Data Pipeline

Automate the loading, validation, and standardisation of heterogeneous Agmarknet market arrival and price datasets.

Objective B: Feature Engineering

Formulate agricultural market features including absolute Price Spread and percentage volatility ratios to quantify risk.

Objective C: Unsupervised ML

Implement Scikit-Learn K-Means clustering with feature standardization and Elbow Method evaluation to isolate market tiers.

Objective D: Interactive UI

Deliver a clean, responsive, full-stack agritech dashboard with real-time 2D PCA visual projections and custom CSV upload support.

🔄 4. System Methodology & Pipeline

Phase 1 – Data Ingestion & Sanitization:

Agmarknet CSV data is parsed, case-insensitive column variations are mapped to standard schemas, currency symbols/commas stripped, and missing prices imputed logically.

Phase 2 – Agricultural Feature Engineering:

Calculates Price_Spread = Max_Price - Min_Price to measure intra-day price dispersion, and Price_Spread_Pct = (Price_Spread / Modal_Price) * 100 to capture relative price volatility.

Phase 3 – Feature Normalization:

Applies StandardScaler to center each feature at zero mean and unit standard deviation, ensuring Euclidean distance in K-Means is not dominated by high-nominal commodities.

Phase 4 – Model Training & Hyperparameter Tuning:

Executes K-Means++ clustering across \(K \in [2, 7]\). Computes Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS) and Silhouette Coefficients to find the mathematical inflection point (Elbow Method).

Phase 5 – Cluster Profiling & Agritech Insights:

Aggregates cluster centroids, computes dominant crops and states, translates mathematical clusters into real-world market archetypes, and renders interactive Chart.js visualizations.

🌟 Advantages & Real-World Impact

  • Empowering Farmers: Enables identification of high-value mandi clusters within transport distance to maximize crop realization.
  • Trader Arbitrage: Detects regional price spreads between surplus and deficit mandi clusters.
  • Policy Buffer Stocks: Informs government agencies (FCI, NAFED) on price stability across clustered regions.
  • Zero Fabrication: All metrics, clusters, and elbow evaluations are calculated directly on genuine market data.

🔭 Limitations & Future Scope

  • Cross-Sectional vs Temporal: Current model clusters daily snapshots. Future work can incorporate time-series clustering (Dynamic Time Warping).
  • Exogenous Variables: Integrating weather patterns, rainfall deficit indices, and diesel freight tariffs.
  • Deep Learning Forecasting: Extending the clustering pipeline with LSTM / Prophet models for 30-day forward price forecasts.
