🌾 Agricultural Market Price Analysis Overview

Addressing information asymmetry and price volatility in fragmented mandi systems

The Challenge: Market Fragmentation

Across India, over 2,400 principal regulated Agricultural Produce Market Committee (APMC) mandis trade daily. Due to transport barriers, local supply shocks, and intermediary margins, identical crops exhibit high price dispersion and volatile spreads.

The MandiCluster Solution

Instead of examining each mandi in isolation, MandiCluster applies Unsupervised Machine Learning (K-Means) to automatically group markets exhibiting cohesive price levels, intraday price spreads, and volatility profiles into interpretable market clusters.

🎯 Key Project Objectives

Core engineering and analytical goals of the MandiCluster architecture

📥

1. Data Standardisation

Automated ingestion, schema validation, and missing value imputation for authentic Agmarknet daily price records.

📐

2. Feature Engineering

Extracting intra-mandi price spreads (Max - Min) and volatility percentages to capture intraday trading risks.

⚖️

3. Preprocessing & Scaling

StandardScaler z-score normalization ensuring fair cluster distance metrics across commodities ranging from ₹1,200/Q to ₹50,000/Q.

🤖

4. Unsupervised Clustering

K-Means++ partition algorithm with Elbow Method and Silhouette Coefficient optimization to segment market price patterns.
