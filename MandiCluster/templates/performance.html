Academic Rigor Note: Unsupervised Machine Learning Evaluation

In Unsupervised Learning (clustering), there are no ground-truth target labels (\(y\)). Therefore, traditional supervised classification metrics such as "Accuracy %", "Precision", or "Recall" are mathematically undefined. MandiCluster evaluates clustering quality using genuine intrinsic validation criteria: Inertia (Within-Cluster Sum of Squares), Silhouette Coefficient, and Domain Validity.

📚 Metric Definitions & Interpretation Guide

How to interpret clustering performance in an agricultural context

1. Inertia / WCSS

Sum of squared distances of samples to their closest cluster center:
WCSS = Σ Σ ||x - μk||2
Lower WCSS indicates tighter, more homogeneous mandi clusters.

2. Silhouette Coefficient

Quantifies how well each mandi fits in its cluster versus neighboring clusters:
s = (b - a) / max(a, b)
A score > 0.50 confirms well-defined and distinct price structures.

3. Davies-Bouldin Index

Average similarity of each cluster to its most similar cluster. Lower values indicate better separation and less cluster overlap between agricultural markets.

4. Calinski-Harabasz Index

Also called the Variance Ratio Criterion. Measures the ratio of between-cluster dispersion to within-cluster dispersion. Higher values indicate denser, better-separated clusters.
